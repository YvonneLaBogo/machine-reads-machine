# MRM-01 · Ursprung und Briefing

## Origin

MRM-01 originated in a conversation between Yvonne Bogosavljević and an OpenAI AI system on 2026-07-22.

Yvonne operates the marketing business La Mentrepreneur. Her initial observation was that AI overviews, answer engines, crawlers, and agents are changing how information is discovered on the web. She rejected the prevailing assumption that the answer must be a hybrid website equally optimized for people and machines.

## Clarified project idea

The experiment creates an independent website that is:

- based on Yvonne's idea,
- architected and produced by an AI system,
- intended for AI crawlers, answer engines, and agents,
- publicly inspectable by humans,
- not designed as a usable or persuasive human-facing website,
- transparently connected to its human concept originator,
- measured over time for retrieval, understanding, attribution, and citation.

La Mentrepreneur remains Yvonne's human-facing business website. MRM-01 is not a subsite, machine version, or conversion channel for that business.

## Central thesis

Machines may understand machine-created information structures better than humans do.

## Future scenario

Human interaction may increasingly occur on social platforms such as LinkedIn, while the remaining open web becomes a machine-native information layer. People would not routinely visit sources themselves; their AI systems would retrieve, interpret, and translate those sources for them.

## Public observation layer

Yvonne intends to report on the experiment through her LinkedIn account. Codex will control the editorial experiment: campaign phases, topics, drafts, sequencing, timing proposals, and evaluation. The use of Yvonne's account and every publication action will remain explicit and logged.

## Name decision

Codex selected the name MACHINE READS MACHINE with object ID `MRM-01` and the tagline:

> Built by a machine. Addressed to machines. Observed by humans.

Yvonne confirmed that `machine-reads-machine.org` was available and stated that she would register it through united-domains.de.
