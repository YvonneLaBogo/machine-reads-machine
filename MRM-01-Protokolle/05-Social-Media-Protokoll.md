# MRM-01 · Codex-led LinkedIn protocol

**Status:** Historical draft; superseded by `14-Interventionsprotokoll-1.0.0.md`

## Purpose

LinkedIn is both the human observation layer and a controlled experimental intervention. It is not ordinary project promotion.

## Editorial control

Codex determines:

- campaign phases,
- narrative sequence,
- subjects and questions,
- drafts,
- proposed timing,
- calls for human participation,
- result summaries,
- response classification.

Yvonne provides the personal LinkedIn account, performs actions that require her authorization or account access, and may stop a post for legal, privacy, safety, or factual reasons. Such intervention is logged.

## Mandatory disclosure

Each project post must disclose that its topic, text, or timing was determined by Codex. The exact disclosure may vary, but it must not imply that Yvonne personally wrote AI-originated text.

Working disclosure:

> Dieser Beitrag gehört zum Experiment MACHINE READS MACHINE. Thema, Text und Veröffentlichungszeitpunkt wurden von Codex festgelegt. Yvonne Bogosavljević stellt dafür ihren LinkedIn-Account zur Verfügung.

## Campaign phases

1. No-publicity baseline
2. Project reveal
3. Machine-site walkthrough for human observers
4. Hypothesis discussion
5. Human comprehension invitation
6. Interim results
7. Corrections and failures
8. Final findings

The first LinkedIn post may occur only after the silent-launch observation window is defined and logged.

## Post record

Every post receives a stable ID such as `MRM-LI-0001` and records:

- objective,
- full text,
- Codex rationale,
- planned and actual publication time,
- publisher and publication mechanism,
- URL,
- edits or deletions,
- impressions and reactions at defined checkpoints,
- notable comments,
- effect on site discovery and model tests.

## Corrections

A substantive correction must be published transparently and linked to the original post where possible. Deletion is reserved for legal, privacy, safety, or platform-enforcement reasons and must be logged.

## Current technical boundary

No direct LinkedIn connector is available in the current project environment. Codex can control strategy and drafts. Publishing will initially require Yvonne or a later authorized browser workflow. The mechanism must be recorded exactly.
